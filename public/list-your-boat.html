Questions

What does it cost to list my boat?
Listing is free and there is no monthly fee. The first 30 owners who sign up with code FOUNDER30 pay 0% commission for 365 days. After that Splash keeps 20% of each booking.
What do you need from me?
Photos of the boat, its basic details, your prices and when it is available. You also set up payouts yourself, because Stripe has to confirm your identity, and you send us the documents needed to verify your account. We tell you exactly which documents apply to your boat.
Can I offer trips with a captain, self-drive rentals, or both?
Yes. You choose how each boat is rented, and you can change it later.
When does my listing go live?
Once your account is verified. Listings we build for you start switched off, so nothing is public until you have checked it and turned it on.
I already list my boat on another site. Can you use that?
Yes. Send us the link and we will build your Splash listing from it, then you review it before it goes live.
