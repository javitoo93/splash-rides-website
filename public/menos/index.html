Menos

Less phone. More life.

Menos turns your iPhone into a simple phone: a calm black screen, the few apps you need in plain text, and everything else truly blocked with Apple's Screen Time.

What it does

  • Allow only the apps you choose. Every other app is blocked while Quiet Mode is on.
  • A black text launcher, plus Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.
  • Call your contacts or dial a number inside Menos.
  • Hide distracting apps, block websites and schedule Quiet Mode.
  • Turning it off means waiting through a countdown you set yourself.

Questions

How do I turn Quiet Mode off?

Open Menos and tap Turn off, then wait for the countdown to finish. If you leave the screen, the countdown starts again. You can change the wait time in Menos's settings.

Why does Menos need Screen Time access?

Apple only lets apps block other apps through Screen Time. Menos asks for access on your own iPhone. Apple never shows Menos which apps you use or for how long.

An app I need is blocked.

Tap Edit apps on the Menos screen and add it to your allowed apps. Removing apps works right away; adding one asks you to wait through the countdown first.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Menos. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, then tap the area under the clock and add Menos.

How does the subscription work?

Menos is free to download. Full access is a yearly subscription, and new subscribers get a free trial first. Payment is taken by Apple when the trial ends, and the subscription renews each year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Menos. To restore on a new iPhone, open Menos and tap Restore on the subscription screen.

How do I remove Menos?

Turn Quiet Mode off first. If you turned on “Prevent deleting apps”, switch it off in Menos's settings, then delete the app as usual.
