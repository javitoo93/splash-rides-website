Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

In short: Menos does not collect, store or share any of your data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Everything you set up in Menos stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

Menos: Minimal Phone (“Menos”) is published by Splash Rides LTD, a company registered in England and Wales. You can contact us at support@splash-inc.com.

Data we collect

None. Menos does not send any information from your device to us or to anyone else, and it contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Screen Time. Menos uses Apple's Screen Time features (Family Controls) to block apps and websites on your own iPhone. The apps and websites you pick are given to Menos by Apple as private tokens that do not reveal their names or your usage. Menos never sees which apps you use or for how long.
  • Contacts. If you allow it, Menos reads your contacts so you can call them from its Phone screen. Your contacts are only read on your iPhone, are not copied, and never leave your device.
  • Settings. Your chosen apps, launcher list, schedule and wait time are saved on your iPhone so Menos and its widgets can work. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Calls

When you call someone from Menos, the call is placed by your iPhone's Phone app. Menos does not record or log your calls.

Children

Menos is designed for adults managing their own iPhone. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Under UK and EU data protection law you still have the right to contact us with questions, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
