Arriba

Wake up. Phone stays locked.

Arriba is an alarm clock for people who snooze, then lie in bed scrolling. It rings like your iPhone's own alarm and keeps the apps you choose locked until you've done a quick wake-up mission.

What it does

  • Rings like the iPhone's own alarm, even in Silent mode and Focus.
  • Wake-up missions: scan a barcode in another room, walk a set number of steps, or solve a few maths problems.
  • Locks the apps you pick with Apple's Screen Time until the mission is done. Calls, Messages and Maps are never locked.
  • Stop only buys you 30 seconds: the alarm rings again until the mission is done.
  • A morning streak, plus Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with your next alarm.

Questions

Why does the alarm ring again after I tap Stop?

iOS always shows a Stop button on alarms. In Arriba, Stop silences the alarm for 30 seconds, then it rings again until you finish your wake-up mission. Tap Start mission instead to open the mission straight away.

How do I unlock my apps in an emergency?

On the mission screen, tap Emergency unlock and wait through the countdown (5 minutes unless you changed it). Your apps unlock and the alarm stops. Calls, Messages and Maps always work, even while other apps are locked.

Why does Arriba need alarm and Screen Time access?

Alarm access lets Arriba ring like the iPhone's own alarm, even in Silent mode. Screen Time access is the only way Apple lets an app lock other apps. Apple never shows Arriba which apps you use or for how long.

Why does Arriba ask for the camera or Motion & Fitness?

Only for the missions that need them: the camera to scan your chosen barcode, and Motion & Fitness to count your steps. Nothing is recorded or saved, and nothing leaves your iPhone.

How do I change the locked apps or the mission?

Open Arriba and go to Settings. There you can change the locked apps, the mission, the number of steps or problems, and the bedtime lock.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Arriba. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, then tap the area under the clock and add Arriba.

How does the subscription work?

Arriba is free to download and needs a yearly subscription to use. New subscribers pay a lower price for their first year, then the regular yearly price. The subscription renews each year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Arriba. To restore on a new iPhone, open Arriba and tap Restore purchase on the subscription screen.
