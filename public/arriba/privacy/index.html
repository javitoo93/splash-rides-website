Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

In short: Arriba does not collect, store or share any of your data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Your alarms, locked apps and wake-up history stay on your iPhone.

Who we are

Arriba: Alarm & App Lock (“Arriba”) is published by Splash Rides LTD, a company registered in England and Wales. You can contact us at support@splash-inc.com.

Data we collect

None. Arriba does not send any information from your device to us or to anyone else, and it contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Alarms. Arriba schedules your alarms with Apple's alarm system on your iPhone. Alarm times and settings are saved on your device only.
  • Screen Time. Arriba uses Apple's Screen Time features (Family Controls) to lock the apps you choose on your own iPhone. Apple gives Arriba those apps as private tokens that do not reveal their names or your usage. Arriba never sees which apps you use or for how long.
  • Camera. If you choose the barcode mission, Arriba uses the camera to read the barcode you picked. Images are processed on your iPhone as you scan and are never saved or sent anywhere. Only the barcode number you register is stored, on your device.
  • Motion & Fitness. If you choose the steps mission, Arriba reads your step count from your iPhone while the mission is open. Steps are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Wake-up history. Your morning streak and the times you got up are saved on your iPhone so Arriba and its widgets can show them. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Arriba is designed for adults managing their own iPhone. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Under UK and EU data protection law you still have the right to contact us with questions, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
